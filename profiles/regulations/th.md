# Regulation checklist: Thailand

Read by `/agent-team:init` in its Regulations step. **This file holds no rate, threshold, deadline or section number, on purpose**: those change, and a copy here would be wrong the day after it was written. It lists what to look up and where, so each project records the current value with its source and date in `.agent/regulations.md`.

## Official sources, in order of authority

| source | holds | address |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Gazette | the law as enacted and every amendment | ratchakitcha.soc.go.th (refused an automated read on 2026-11-10: the human may have to open it) |
| Revenue Department | tax rates, forms, e-Tax Invoice and e-Receipt rules, rulings | rd.go.th |
| Personal Data Protection Committee Office (PDPC) | the PDPA, its notifications and guidelines | pdpc.or.th |
| Social Security Office | contribution rates and the wage ceiling | sso.go.th |

A law firm's or vendor's summary is a lead to the official text, never the source recorded.

## Topics to look up, by what the project does

Take only the rows that apply. For each, record the current value, the date it took effect, and any change already announced with its start date.

| the project | look up |
|---|---|
| issues invoices or receipts | VAT rate and whether a temporary rate is in force and until when; what a tax invoice must contain; e-Tax Invoice and e-Receipt requirements; rounding of tax; document numbering and retention period |
| pays suppliers or contractors | withholding tax rates by type of income; the certificate and filing required |
| runs payroll | personal income tax brackets and allowances; withholding on salary; social security contribution rate and wage ceiling for employer and employee; provident fund rules if used; minimum wage where relevant |
| stores personal data | lawful bases for collection; what consent must look like and how it is withdrawn; data subject rights and the time allowed to answer; breach notification, to whom and how fast; when a data protection officer is required; cross-border transfer rules; retention |
| stores sensitive personal data (health, biometrics, criminal records and the other listed categories) | the current list of categories; the consent rule and its exceptions, including for employment |
| sends marketing messages | consent and opt-out rules for the channel used |

## When a value cannot be confirmed

Write the row with `status: unverified` and what was tried. An agent that needs an unverified row returns `NEEDS-PM`. Never fill a gap from memory or from a summary site.
